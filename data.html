Data dictionary

data_monthStatistical month, YYYY-MM.
series_idSeries identifier, e.g. ETRX-CN-85.
rateEffective rate: calculated duty ÷ customs value. Decimal (0.0713 = 7.13%), six places.
rate_dutiableCalculated duty ÷ dutiable value. Empty when dutiable value is zero.
duty_free_share1 − dutiable value ÷ customs value.
con_valCustoms value of imports for consumption, US dollars.
cal_dutCalculated duty, US dollars.
dut_valDutiable value, US dollars.
vintage_dateDate this value was printed (UTC).
vintage_notebackfill (built retroactively; embeds Census revisions), live (first print on release), or revision.

Data products

Beyond the free index, the same pipeline produces three things professionals ask for:

Full-granularity feedEvery HS chapter × the top 60 origins, monthly since 2010 — about 6,000 series, updated the day each print lands. Sample: Vietnam, all chapters (CSV).
Custom cuts and client indicesAny origin × HS2/HS4/HS6/HS10 series, full history, delivered as CSV, JSON and a credited chart — usually same day. Or an import-mix-weighted index on a client's own origin × product shares, rebuilt every print. Useful for entry-level duty histories in refund and customs work, and for measuring one company's exposure rather than the nation's.
Settlement and benchmark licensingUse of the index as the reference of a listed, cleared or OTC product, with calculation-agent services under the rulebook's calendar, fallback and revision policy.

To request a cut, a client index, feed access or licensing terms, write to press@etrxindex.com with the origins, products and period you need. Academic and think-tank use of the feed is free with citation.
